Rotation Reminder

Privacy Policy & Data Transparency

100% Offline

🛡️ Foundational Privacy Commitment

Rotation Reminder stores all alarms, weight logs, BMI profiles, and custom goals 100% locally on your device using encrypted local storage. Zero personal, health, or tracking data is collected, transmitted, or shared with external servers.

🔒 Local-First Data Storage

All information you enter into Rotation Reminder—including your name, height, body weight logs, alarm schedules, habit completions, and security PIN—remains strictly inside your phone’s local sandboxed storage (using @capacitor/preferences with encrypted local storage fallback).

The app operates without any remote backend, user authentication server, or cloud database. We do not maintain any user accounts or access keys. If you uninstall the app or wipe its data via Android system settings, all local data is permanently cleared from your device.

🚫 Zero Analytics, Trackers, or Ads

Rotation Reminder is completely ad-free and tracking-free:

  • ✓
    No Advertising Networks: Zero ad SDKs, banner ads, or tracking identifiers (no Google AdMob, Unity, or third-party ad networks).
  • ✓
    No Analytics or Telemetry: Zero analytics SDKs (no Firebase Analytics, Mixpanel, or telemetry daemons).
  • ✓
    No Third-Party Data Brokers: Your health, schedule, and weight progress are never sold, rented, or monetized in any form.

⚙️ Android Device Permissions & Usage Rationale

The app requests only the minimal Android permissions necessary to perform its alarm and pacer functions:

  • •
    POST_NOTIFICATIONS & SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM: Required to trigger timely audio chimes and notifications for scheduled rotations and habits even when the device is asleep or locked.
  • •
    VIBRATE: Used to provide tactile feedback and alert patterns during interval alarms and pacing intervals.
  • •
    WAKE_LOCK: Used strictly during the active 40x Pacer countdown to prevent your phone screen from timing out while you perform physical seated rotations.
  • •
    USE_BIOMETRIC (Optional): Used only if you enable the optional App Lock. Biometric authentication (fingerprint / face) is handled directly by Android OS system APIs. Biometric data is never accessible to, handled by, or stored by the app.

📦 User Data Ownership, Backup & Deletion

You maintain unconditional ownership and control over your data:

  • ✓
    Export Backup: Export your entire database (alarms, weight history, body profile) to an offline JSON file anytime via Profile → Backup & Restore.
  • ✓
    Import Backup: Migrate your data seamlessly to a new phone without relying on cloud services.
  • ✓
    Instant Factory Reset: Tap "Reset All App Data" in the settings menu to completely purge all records from local device memory.

👶 Children's Privacy (COPPA & GDPR Compliance)

Rotation Reminder is a personal health and wellness interval timer. It does not collect any personal information from any user, including children under the age of 13.
