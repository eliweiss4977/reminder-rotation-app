Privacy Policy & Data Transparency

🔒 100% Offline & Local Storage

Rotation Reminder is designed with absolute privacy as a foundational principle. All personal data—including your name, height, body weight logs, alarm schedules, habit completions, and security PIN—is stored exclusively on your device using local storage and encrypted device preferences.

We operate no external servers, no databases, and no cloud backups. Your data never leaves your device unless you choose to export it manually.

🚫 Zero Analytics, Trackers, or Ads

The app contains zero analytics SDKs, zero advertising networks, and zero tracking cookies. We do not track your usage patterns, location, device identifiers, or habits.

⚙️ Device Permissions Explained

  • •
    Notifications & Exact Alarms: Used strictly to ring and vibrate your scheduled 40x acupressure rotation reminders and daily habits at the precise times you configured, even when the screen is locked.
  • •
    Vibration & Audio: Used to play synthesized chime tones and haptic feedback during countdowns and alarms.
  • •
    Biometrics (Fingerprint / Face Unlock): Used exclusively to unlock the optional App Lock. Biometric authentication is handled entirely by your device operating system; the app never receives or stores your biometric template.

📦 Full User Data Ownership & Portability

You have complete ownership of your data at all times:

  • ✓ Export JSON: Download a full offline backup of all your alarms, weight history, and settings at any time.
  • ✓ Import JSON: Restore your data on a new device with zero friction.
  • ✓ Delete All Data: Wipe 100% of local storage and reset the app with a single tap in My Profile.
