Privacy Policy

🔒 100% Offline & Local Storage

All data (height, weight logs, alarm schedules, habit completions, and security PIN) is saved exclusively on your device. Zero cloud servers or analytics tracking.

⚙️ Device Permissions

Notifications & Exact Alarms are used strictly to sound your 40x acupressure rotation reminders. Biometrics are processed only by your device's operating system.

📦 Data Ownership

You can export your complete data as JSON or permanently wipe all data anytime from My Profile.
